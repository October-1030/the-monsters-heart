## 0. Locked Canons & Delivery Rules (must NOT be violated by the drafter)

1. **LOCKED VOW (exact wording, every occurrence):**
   - Full true vow: *"Let one bear all sorrow so the many may live. And the one who bears shall not bear alone."*
   - Corrupted temple version (rehearsed Ch 3, carved on door Ch 9): *"Let one devour all sorrow so the many may live."* — "bear" was corrupted into "devour".
   - Final line is withheld until Ch 30: *"And the one who bears shall not bear alone."*
2. **LOCKED TITHE RULES (magic spec):**
   - The Devourer can eat **only** grief that is *offered or abandoned* — never grief that is *held*. This is why kingdoms had to be ritually "delivered" via tithe-shrines.
   - Each meal costs him **memory and feeling** (visible forgetting = Chekhov's gun; he loses the middle of his own name in Ch 12, recovers "Maren" in Ch 27).
   - Starvation pools grief into the world as the **plague of sorrow** (grief-plague lands, Ch 7).
   - Elowen's sanctity cracks his numbness → **touching him makes him remember how to hurt** (tenderness is literally his pain).
   - Undoing the curse = the weight is **shared**, never replaced; someone must *choose* to share it (Ch 30, 33–34).
3. **LOCKED POV:** Single close-third, Elowen. Maren's past arrives as *narration she hears* — he tells her, or she reads it in his pain — never a separate POV.
4. **LOCKED ROMANCE BEATS:** Almost-moments at **Ch 15, Ch 22, Ch 28, Ch 33** (≥3 before first kiss ✓) · First kiss at **Ch 38** (~95%, after the 60% rule) · Every intimacy is plot-crisis-driven, never page-count-driven.
5. **LOCKED PACING:** Action 5 : Emotion 3 : Worldbuilding 2 per chapter block; no three consecutive monotone chapters; respite beats at **Ch 14, Ch 22, Ch 28b, Ch 35**.
6. **LOCKED ENDING:** HEA — both alive, together, free; grief shared, not destroyed. Matriarch's curse ("You'll become the next me") is *answered* in the Final Image, not sequel-baited.
7. **THEME (stated Ch 2, paid off Ch 36–40):** Grief is not to be devoured or destroyed — it is to be *shared*. The true monster is any institution that uses one person's pain to keep the many comfortable.
8. **Two-of-three scene rule:** every scene advances plot **and** deepens relationship **and/or** discloses world.
9. **Hook rotation rule:** no hook type repeats more than 2 consecutive chapters (final three chapters are the designed resolution exception, see C).

**Timeline (for Continuity Editor):** Day 1–3 temple · Day 4–6 road · Day 7 tithe-shrine · Day 9–10 swallowed kingdom/Heart · Day 11–14 first lessons · Day 15 sisterhood arrives · Day 19 midpoint · Day 22–23 siege I · Day 25–26 dark night + choice · Day 27 final siege + transfer · Day 28–29 climax · Day 29 night first kiss · Days 40–60 new community · ~2 months later final image.

## 0b. Structural Rulings & Discrepancy Notes（结构裁决与差异说明）

### 0b.1 Chapter count: 40, not 30（章节数裁决）
The relay summary stated "30 chapters · first kiss Ch25 (83%)". The **published Three-Act Structure document (monsters_heart_three_act_structure.md) is authoritative** and specifies **40 chapters** (Act I Ch1–10 / Act II Ch11–30 / Act III Ch31–40, first kiss **Ch38** ≈ 95%). This outline follows the published document. The "30 chapters" figure in the handoff message is a summary compression error; **all downstream roles (Continuity Editor, Native Line Editor, drafters) must use the 40-chapter numbering** and the Ch38 first kiss. Romance-beat page-position percentages quoted in the handoff (83%) are likewise superseded by the architect's own table (95%).

### 0b.2 Chapter-mode pacing deviation — flagged with reason（节奏偏差说明）
At the chapter-primary-mode level this outline distributes **Action 12 : Emotion 18 : Worldbuilding 10** (Act I: A3/E2/W5 · Act II: A6/E8/W4 · Act III: A3/E8/W1), which leans emotional rather than the architect's scene-level ratio Action 5 : Emotion 3 : Worldbuilding 2. This is **deliberate and recommended**, not an error:
1. The C&R Designer's 6-beat slow-burn and the Emotional Promise ("cry, then feel held") require interiority at the romance beats (Ch19, Ch28, Ch33, Ch38).
2. The architect's scene cards mark nearly every major scene as **A/E or A/W** (two-of-three rule), so the 5:3:2 ratio lives at the scene-content level, not the chapter level.
3. Worldbuilding is concentrated where it must be (Acts I–II: shrine, ledger, vow) and drip-fed thereafter (Ch12–13, 17, 24, 26).
**Drafter instruction:** keep action scenes dual-purpose (advance plot **and** deepen relationship or disclose world); never add info-dumps in Act III.

### 0b.3 Locked canon reaffirmation（锁定规则重申）
Vow wording, tithe rules, touch rule, sanctity rule, romance beats, and HEA are locked per §0. Any conflict found during drafting must be escalated to Story Architect; do not silently alter canon.

## B. Scene Beat Breakdowns (40 chapters · 2–3 scenes each)

> Format per chapter: **Scene # (two-of-three tags)** → beats → *emotional arc (from → to)*. All beats are what the drafter must hit; dialogue lines in quotes are locked or near-locked.

### ACT I — THE SAINT (Ch 1–10) · "She was a weapon who thought she was loved."

**Ch 1 — The Garden of the Sealed** *(Day 1, dawn–night · 2,400 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** Temple garden at dawn. Elowen tends herbs, performs the Saint's morning rite flawlessly. Grief-voices of the dead whisper under the birdsong — she counts them, hides her flinch. A novice asks if she hears anything; she lies smoothly. → *Serene → secretly haunted.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** Sisterhood meal. Sable (squad leader) teases her; Niamh watches too closely. Elowen's mask of perfection cracks for one instant when a swallowed child's voice surfaces. Niamh notices, says nothing. → *Haunted → vigilant.*
- **Scene 3 (W+E):** Night. Alone, Elowen prays the ending prayer for the Devourer (corrupted vow, first pass) and feels the words *wrong* in her mouth. Beneath the voices of the dead, one voice is different — alive, tired, vast. *"He is starving."* → *Vigilant → disturbed.*
- **Hook (S):** The grief-voices surge: "He is starving."

**Ch 2 — The Page With No Word** *(Day 1, afternoon · 2,300 w)*
- **Scene 1 (W+E):** Archive. Niamh "teaches" Elowen the history of the Devourer — the swallowed kingdoms, the plague of sorrow. Theme stated: *"Grief is not an enemy to be killed. It is a weight to be shared."* Niamh's hand trembles on a page. → *Curious → unsettled.*
- **Scene 2 (A+W):** Niamh almost reveals too much ("the vow was…"), catches herself, hides a page in her sleeve. Elowen sees a scratched-out word on the exposed line — the shape under it could be "bear". → *Unsettled → doubting.*
- **Hook (S):** The page has a scratched-out word — and Niamh will not speak it.

**Ch 3 — The Hesitation** *(Day 1, evening · 2,300 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** Prayer hall. Elowen rehearses the ending prayer before the sisterhood; the corrupted vow rings in the marble: *"Let one devour all sorrow so the many may live."* She performs it perfectly; inside, the words scrape. → *Dutiful → doubtful.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** The Matriarch coaches her alone. For a heartbeat the Matriarch hesitates mid-sentence — as if the vow hurts her too — then smooths it over with love: "You are the key, child. The blade the world needs." Elowen basks in the approval; the doubt is buried. → *Doubtful → reassured (buried).* 
- **Hook (E):** The Matriarch hesitated. What does *she* know?

**Ch 4 — The Mission (Catalyst)** *(Day 2, morning · 2,500 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** Great Hall, full assembly. The Matriarch proclaims the mission: Elowen will go to the Devourer's heart and end him. Crowd ecstasy; Elowen's elevation. A Remnant beggar at the door mouths "Don't" before guards drag him off. → *Elevated → shaken.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** Blessing ritual. The Matriarch anoints Elowen; the sisterhood swears to await her triumph. Sable is assigned as her shield. Elowen touches the blade meant for the monster — it feels like the only identity she has. → *Shaken → resolved.*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** That night, in the forbidden language only she knows (the dead-speech), a tired, gentle voice speaks inside her skull: *"Don't come. I don't want to be ended."* The mission now has a face. → *Resolved → terrified.*
- **Hook (S):** The monster speaks to her — and he is polite, and sad, and afraid.

**Ch 5 — The Charm (Debate I)** *(Day 2, night · 2,300 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+E):** Her cell. Elowen argues with herself — the voice could be a trap, the Devil's work; the temple said the Devourer has no voice. The Matriarch visits, weaponizes love and honor: "The world waits on you, my Saint." Elowen's free will bends. → *Torn → pressured.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** Niamh comes at midnight with a small cloth charm (warm, alive, wrong). *"If the temple asks you to give something, don't."* She will not explain. Elowen takes it, hides it. → *Pressured → unsettled.*
- **Hook (E):** Niamh's gift — and her fear.

**Ch 6 — The Ledger (Debate II)** *(Day 3, pre-dawn · 2,300 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** Forbidden shelf in the archive. Elowen steals a look at sealed scrolls: tithe records, shrine manifests, a bound ledger. The Matriarch catches her — a cold warning, not a punishment: "Curiosity is the first heresy, child." → *Curious → guilty.*
- **Scene 2 (A+W):** After the Matriarch leaves, Elowen glimpses one page of the ledger: kingdoms as entries in a **three-hundred-year cycle**, each with a date and a seal. The last date is inked, not printed. → *Guilty → chilled.*
- **Hook (S):** The tithe ledger — a cycle older than the temple's story.

**Ch 7 — The Girl With the Dead Dog** *(Day 4–6 · 2,400 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** The road; first kingdom of the grief-plague. Gray villages, sorrow-mists, people who have forgotten how to weep — the plague of sorrow. Elowen performs blessings; they don't help. → *Determined → horrified.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** A girl weeps over a dead dog — her grief is *held*, not taken. Why is she spared the numbness? Sable dismisses it; Elowen files the anomaly. The girl's voice joins Elowen's private chorus — not grief, but love-mourning. → *Horrified → questioning.*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** Night. Tithe-shrine bells ring in the village — the sorrow-offering is being collected. Elowen watches from the inn window, unsettled by how *eager* the bell-ringer sounds. → *Questioning → uneasy.*
- **Hook (C):** The shrine bells — and the girl's grief that was *not* taken.

**Ch 8 — The Shrine That Feeds** *(Day 7 · 2,400 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** Tithe-shrine. Elowen witnesses a sorrow-offering rite: villagers lay their grief in the shrine's basin; the priest "seals" it; the sorrow visibly flows *downward*, into the crypt — not purified, *drained*. Elowen's blessing-habit revolts. → *Horrified → suspicious.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** Niamh's charm warms against her chest as she nears the crypt door — as if it knows what's below. Sable urges her on; the priest's smile is too bright. → *Suspicious → alarmed.*
- **Hook (S):** The charm warms toward the crypt — what is being fed down there?

**Ch 9 — The Door With the Scratched Vow** *(Day 8–9 · 2,500 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** The swallowed kingdom — the Devourer's territory. Gray silence, memory-voices roaring in Elowen's head (the dead of three kingdoms). Sable wants to turn back; Elowen forces herself forward, the only one who can *hear*. → *Uneasy → steeled.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** They reach the final door, carved with the vow. The top line reads *"Let one devour all sorrow so the many may live"* — but beneath the newer letters, original words are scratched out. Elowen runs her fingers over the gouges: she can almost read *"bear"*. → *Steeled → shaken.*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** The door opens by itself. Sable draws her blade; Elowen steps through alone. The voice inside is close now, breathing. → *Shaken → resolved.*
- **Hook (C):** The scratched-out original — and the door that opened by itself.

**Ch 10 — The Man in the Heart (First Act Turn / R1)** *(Day 10, dusk · 2,500 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** The Heart: a vast cavern of ash and faint light, grief pooled like water. No monster — a man, kneeling, weeping in the forbidden language. He is soft-spoken, gaunt, ancient; he knows her name. → *Armed → disarmed.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** She draws her blade. He does not defend himself. He speaks her language — the dead-speech is also *his* language: "I have heard you since you were a child. You were the only voice that wasn't grief." Her hand shakes. → *Disarmed → paralyzed.*
- **Scene 3 (A+E):** He offers no fight, no plea for mercy — only *"Please. Either end me, or stay. I can't bear being alone again."* She cannot strike. She sheathes the blade. → *Paralyzed → broken-open.*
- **Hook (E):** "Either end me, or stay." She stayed.

### ACT II — THE MONSTER (Ch 11–30) · "The longer she stayed, the more the heresy felt like home."

**Ch 11 — The Stalemate** *(Day 10–11 · 2,300 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+E):** The Heart, first hours. Silence. She cannot kill him; she cannot leave (the door won't open for her alone — or she won't make it open). He brings her water in a cracked cup; his hands shake. → *Disarmed → confused.*
- **Scene 2 (A+W):** He tells her what he eats, matter-of-factly: *offered or abandoned* grief, never held. He hasn't been fed in years — the plague spreads when he starves. She doesn't believe him. → *Confused → suspicious.*
- **Scene 3 (A+E):** Night. He speaks to the dark, forgetting she's there: *"I remember who I was. I remember the vow. I remember — the temple lied."* → *Suspicious → rattled.*
- **Hook (S):** "The temple lied."

**Ch 12 — Learning to Hear (R2a)** *(Day 12 · 2,300 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** He teaches her to *hear* grief — not the dead's voices, but the world's: the river mourning a drowned child, the forest grieving a burned grove, the swallowed villages singing under the ash. Her gift expands; so does her doubt. → *Confused → awed.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** She asks his name. He opens his mouth — and forgets the middle of it. "Ma— … ren? No. Ma—" He laughs, hollow. She watches him lose himself in real time. → *Awed → aching.*
- **Scene 3 (W+E):** That night she hears the girl with the dead dog's grief carried on the wind — *held* grief, traveling. The tithe-shrines are not "sealing" sorrow; they are *moving* it. → *Aching → suspicious.*
- **Hook (E):** He forgets the middle of his own name — and doesn't notice.

**Ch 13 — The True Vow (R2b)** *(Day 13 · 2,500 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** Confrontation. She recites the corrupted vow; he flinches as if struck. He speaks the true one: *"Let one bear all sorrow so the many may live."* "Bear," he says. "The temple made it *devour*." She demands proof. → *Suspicious → shattered.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** Proof arrives as memory: he shows her (through shared grief-hearing) the founding day — the first chosen one, the original vow, the temple's slow corruption of the word. She sees him young, before the hunger. → *Shattered → gutted.*
- **Scene 3 (A+E):** He sleeps from exhaustion. She stands over him with her blade — mission, identity, the Matriarch's love all in her hand. She lowers it. She sits on the floor beside him. → *Gutted → tender (heretical).*
- **Hook (S):** She held the blade over him — and chose not to end him.

**Ch 14 ◆ Respite — The Only Voice That Wasn't Grief** *(Day 14 · 2,300 w)*
- **Scene 1 (E+W):** Quiet evening. No blades between them. He tells her what it's like to eat sorrow: each meal costs a memory, a feeling; he has forgotten his mother's face, the smell of rain, the sound of his own laugh. He remembers *her* voice from her childhood — the only voice that wasn't grief. → *Shattered → tender.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** Boundary-crossing conversation: she asks what he wants. "Oblivion," he says, and then, softer: "…No. I want to stop being the monster." She almost says *you're not* — stops herself. The almost-words hang. → *Tender → warm.*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** A horn sounds at the territory's edge — the temple's war-horn. Sable's arrival. The warmth shatters. → *Warm → trapped.*
- **Hook (C):** Sable's horn at the territory's edge.

**Ch 15 — The Sisterhood Arrives (Almost-Moment #1)** *(Day 15 · 2,500 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** Sable and the temple squad arrive at the territory edge — "support" for the kill. Elowen hides the truth; Sable's eyes are already wrong (kill-order in them). Tension between loyalty and the man behind her. → *Trapped → guarded.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** Night. Almost-moment #1: Elowen stands in his shadow, hand raised toward it — a phantom touch, a wish to comfort. He turns; she drops her hand. Neither speaks. The air is thick with what wasn't said. → *Guarded → aching.*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** Sable delivers "good news": the victory feast is already planned; the temple expects Elowen's triumphant return within a month. The trap closes around her freedom. → *Aching → trapped.*
- **Hook (E):** Sable's "good news" — and the kill-order behind her smile.

**Ch 16 — The Child's Grief** *(Day 16, night · 2,500 w)*

- **Scene 2 (A+E):** Elowen stops Sable — and in the struggle, Sable *hears* it: a swallowed child's grief, raw, unbearable. Sable breaks. She drops the blade, weeping, and for the first time doubts the mission aloud. → *Protective → rocked.*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** A soldier collapses at the camp edge — plague (the sorrow-plague) enters the camp. The Devourer's starvation has consequences at their own door. → *Rocked → alarmed.*
- **Hook (C):** A soldier collapses — plague inside the temple's own camp.

**Ch 17 — The Remnant and the Ledger** *(Day 17, night · 2,500 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** The Remnant find them — survivors who venerate the Devourer as a god of mercy: "He ate our grief. Someone took it, so we could live." They bring evidence: tithe ledgers, burned records, shrine manifests. → *Alarmed → rocked.*
- **Scene 2 (A+W):** The ledgers prove it: the temple *delivered* their kingdoms — the shrines collected sorrow, the temple fed the Devourer whole populations, then blamed his "appetite" for the plagues it caused. Famine, plague, war — all covered by his legend. → *Rocked → sickened.*
- **Scene 3 (A+E):** The last ledger page. Elowen's name, inked fresh, with the seal of the Matriarch: **the thirteenth "key"** in the three-hundred-year cycle. She is not the blade that ends it. She is the next meal. → *Sickened → gutted.*
- **Hook (S):** Her name is on the last page of the ledger.

**Ch 18 — The Kill-Order** *(Day 18 · 2,400 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+E):** She confronts Sable about the kill-order. Sable reveals it: *if Elowen hesitates, Sable is to execute the Saint and complete the rite herself.* The sisterhood was never coming to save her. → *Gutted → defiant.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** She tells the man in the Heart the truth about the ledger. He is not surprised: "Every time I starve, they deliver a kingdom. I eat. I forget. They call me monster. Repeat." She swears she will not let it happen — to him, or to the next name on the page. → *Defiant → determined.*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** Distant war-horns — not Sable's squad. The temple army is coming, early, to "complete the rite" — to feed him, or to burn them all. → *Determined → grim.*
- **Hook (C):** The temple army comes — not to save, to complete.

**Ch 19 — The Midpoint Double-Reversal (R3)** *(Day 19 · 2,500 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** Chaos. Sable moves to execute the order — blade at Elowen's throat, or at his; the camp erupts. Elowen shields him with her body. → *Determined → desperate.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** Her hand touches his skin — his bare arm. He crumples: feeling floods back, tenderness *hurts* (the curse made literal). His pain pours into her through the contact; she tastes the swallowed grief of three kingdoms — every meal, every forgotten face. → *Desperate → devastated.*
- **Scene 3 (A+E):** She holds him anyway, through the flood. Reversal A: he is real, he suffers, tenderness wounds him. Reversal B: the temple is the true monster, and she is its instrument — the key *and* the offering. She understands her whole life in one gutted breath. → *Devastated → broken-open.*
- **Hook (E):** Double reversal: he is real; the temple is the monster; she is the instrument.

**Ch 20 — Looking for Undo** *(Day 19–20 · 2,300 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+E):** Aftermath. She breathes; she tends his shaking hands; she learns the touch-rule (he can bear her touch now only in small doses). He won't accept "free you" — "undo" might mean *end*; the curse may be all that keeps him alive. → *Broken-open → stubborn.*
- **Scene 2 (A+W):** She opens the Remnant's forbidden books — old shrine records, the first chosen one's fragments, rite diagrams. She searches for *undo*, not *end*: a transfer, a sharing, a way to lift the weight. → *Stubborn → hopeful.*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** The books hint at a ritual and a final line of the vow — but the last page is missing. Someone (Niamh? the Matriarch?) cut it out. → *Hopeful → determined.*
- **Hook (S):** The missing page — and the word "share" inked in the margin.

**Ch 21 — The Vanguard** *(Day 21 · 2,500 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** The temple vanguard attacks the territory edge. Elowen, Sable (half-defected), and the Remnant hold a thin line. Battle beats: arrows, sorrow-mist, the first deaths. → *Determined → fierce.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** The vanguard reads the kill-orders aloud: "The Saint is corrupted — purge." Elowen hears her name become a curse. She fights with the blade meant for him — now used *for* him. → *Fierce → savage.*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** A captured herald carries the Matriarch's decree, named: *purge* — not rescue, not redemption. The temple will burn the heretic Saint and the monster together. → *Savage → grim.*
- **Hook (C):** The decree: purge.

**Ch 22 ◆ Respite — Firelight and the Unanswered Question (Almost-Moment #2)** *(Day 22 · 2,400 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** Firelight strategy session: defenses, the army's approach, the transfer ritual's requirements. His past assembles piece by piece: he was the *first chosen one* — the temple's first experiment, before the corruption. → *Grim → focused.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** Almost-moment #2. The fire dims. He asks, carefully, as if the question might break him: *"If I were a man again — would you still listen?"* Her breath stops. Her hand moves toward his — stops. She doesn't answer. → *Focused → aching.*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** She changes the subject to the siege plans; the question stays between them, unanswered and loud. → *Aching → frightened.*
- **Hook (E):** She doesn't answer — the question frightens her more than the army.

**Ch 23 — Siege I: First Blood by His Hand** *(Day 23 · 2,500 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** The assault breaks on the territory. Elowen and Sable defend; the Remnant fight with shrine-tools. The line bends. → *Focused → ruthless.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** Maren acts: he "sends" grief as a weapon — the stored sorrow of the swallowed kingdoms poured at the attackers like a wave. It works; it is terrible. First blood by his hand. He weeps afterward. → *Ruthless → horrified (for him).*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** A dying officer, a temple man, grabs Elowen's wrist: *"You're not saving him. You're completing his hunger."* She doesn't understand — yet. → *Horrified → sickened.*
- **Hook (C):** The officer's words — completing his hunger.

**Ch 24 — The Cycle** *(Day 24 · 2,400 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** Prisoner tent. She interrogates the captured officer, who breaks: the cycle is *voluntary* — the Devourer starves on a schedule the temple sets; the temple feeds him a kingdom; he eats; his memory erodes; he forgets himself; repeat. The plagues are *timed* to his hunger. → *Sickened → furious.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** She returns to him with the truth, rage and grief tangled. He confirms it quietly: he knew. He has known for centuries. He chose the forgetting over the world drowning. → *Furious → gutted.*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** She demands the whole vow — the final line that might free him. He refuses. "You don't know the vow's last line," he says, "because if you did, you'd try to die for me." → *Gutted → furious (again).*
- **Hook (S):** "You don't know the vow's last line."

**Ch 25 — The First Real Fight** *(Day 24, night · 2,400 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+E):** The Heart. Their first real fight: she demands the last line; he refuses; trust cracks. She accuses him of wanting to die; he accuses her of wanting to be the Saint again. Cruel words, both true. → *Furious → wounded.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** She walks out. In the camp, Sable — searching Elowen's pack for answers — finds Niamh's note folded inside the charm. It is addressed to Elowen, in Niamh's hand. → *Wounded → alarmed.*
- **Hook (E):** Sable finds Niamh's note inside the charm.

**Ch 26 — Niamh's Truth** *(Day 25, pre-dawn · 2,400 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** The note. Niamh's truth-letter: Elowen was the *thirteenth* chosen — the temple has been selecting "keys" for three hundred years; each was groomed as the pure Saint, each was fed to the Devourer as the next meal; Elowen's entire identity is a lie built to make her walk willingly into the cycle. → *Alarmed → gutted.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** She confronts him: *"Why didn't you tell me you knew I was chosen?"* His silence is the answer. He knew from the moment he heard her childhood voice; he said nothing because saying it would make her leave — and he couldn't bear being alone again. Both of them are broken by this. → *Gutted → furious → hollow.*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** She realizes: the charm's warmth wasn't a compass to the crypt — it was Niamh's grief, given to her, *held* for her. Niamh loved her enough to break the vow of silence. → *Hollow → held (faint).*
- **Hook (S):** "Why didn't you tell me you knew I was chosen?" — silence.

**Ch 27 — Dark Night I: Maren** *(Day 25, night · 2,400 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+E):** She nearly runs — walks to the territory edge, ready to vanish, ready to let Sable end him and go home to the lie. The world is safer that way. She almost believes it. → *Hollow → despairing.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** He finds her there. He doesn't persuade, doesn't beg, doesn't argue — he just sits beside her in the dark. Then: *"I've thought about ending too. Every day. But you were never my reason to end."* → *Despairing → held.*
- **Scene 3 (A+E):** She asks his name — the real one, the one the forgetting took. He searches, finds it, gives it to her like a wound: **Maren.** She says it back. It fits. → *Held → broken-open.*
- **Hook (E):** She asks his name. He tells her: Maren.

**Ch 28 ◆ Dark Night II: It's Worth It (R4)** *(Day 25–26 · 2,400 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+E):** The Heart, first light. Almost-moment #3 → completed differently: she reaches for him — and stops, afraid of hurting him. He takes her hand himself and bears the pain of the touch, his face twisting, his voice steady: *"It's worth it."* → *Broken-open → devastated (tender).*
- **Scene 2 (E):** They both cry — her grief for her stolen life, his grief for everything he forgot. She holds his hand through it. Neither speaks. The world's grief and theirs, together, in a small warm space. → *Devastated → held.*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** A scout bursts in: purge-fire lights on the horizon — the Matriarch's army has begun the final assault; they will arrive by dawn. → *Held → steeled.*
- **Hook (C):** Purge-fire on the horizon — the final assault comes at dawn.

**Ch 29 — The Impossible Path** *(Day 26 · 2,300 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+E):** Council of war with Sable (now fully defected), the Remnant, and Maren. Options are exhausted: fight and die, or the ritual. Elowen states it plainly: she will become the new bearer — take the grief into herself, share the weight, break his curse. At cost of her life, or her self. → *Steeled → resolute.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** Maren refuses — flatly, furiously, desperately: it may kill her; he won't let her suffer what he suffered. She doesn't argue. She just says: *"I need the last line. To save you, not kill you."* → *Resolute → aching.*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** Sable's scouts report the army's position; hours remain. The ritual's requirements are checked: two speakers, one vow, a chosen heart. → *Aching → grim.*
- **Hook (S):** "I need the last line. To save you, not kill you."

**Ch 30 — The Last Line (Second Act Turn)** *(Day 26 · 2,500 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+E):** He tells her the vow's final line — the one the temple cut out of every book: *"And the one who bears shall not bear alone."* The curse was designed for sharing from the beginning. Undoing it means someone *chooses to share the weight* — not replace it. → *Grim → stunned.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** He still refuses: sharing may kill her; he would rather burn than lose her. She overrides him — not with argument, with choice. Blade back in sheath, she meets his eyes: *"I choose you."* → *Stunned → decided.*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** The temple army breaches the territory's outer line. Elowen — no longer the Saint, no longer a weapon — draws the blade meant for him and turns it toward the gate. → *Decided → fierce.*
- **Hook (E):** Blade back in sheath: "I choose you." — and the army at the gate.

### ACT III — THE HEART (Ch 31–40) · "The monster was never him."

**Ch 31 — The Matriarch Comes** *(Day 27 · 2,500 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** Siege II: the territory's gate. Elowen, Sable, the Remnant, and Maren's limited power hold the line against the temple army. Blood, sorrow-mist, falling. → *Fierce → grim.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** The Matriarch arrives in person — radiant, terrible, unhurried. No negotiation, no mercy: she offers only the old bargain. Elowen sees the woman who raised her and the monster who fed her — both, at once. → *Grim → anguished.*
- **Scene 3 (A+E):** The Matriarch's parting words to Elowen: *"You'll die before you become what I am."* A curse, a warning, a confession. → *Anguished → defiant.*
- **Hook (C):** "You'll die before you become what I am."

**Ch 32 — The Origin of the Lie** *(Day 27 · 2,500 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** Battlefield, before the Matriarch. Elowen refuses the old bargain one last time — aloud, before both armies: she will not slay him, and she will not be fed to him. → *Defiant → resolute.*
- **Scene 2 (A+W):** The Matriarch, enraged, reveals the temple's origin: the founders, overwhelmed by grief after the first great plague, chose "one swallows" because "all share" was too hard. The corruption was the original sin — the temple has been feeding the lie ever since. → *Resolute → sickened.*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** The Matriarch's order: burn the bearer — *and her grief*. The purge-fire is primed to burn away Elowen's burden, which means burning away the world's grief, everyone's emotional memory. The real weapon is unveiled. → *Sickened → horrified.*
- **Hook (C):** The Matriarch's order: burn the bearer — and her grief.

**Ch 33 — The Transfer Begins (R5a)** *(Day 27, night · 2,500 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+E):** The rite. Elowen and Maren in the Heart, hands joined, the vow spoken. He fights it — tries to pull away, to save her from the cost; she overrides him with her sanctity, the key that was never a blade. → *Defiant → intimate.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** The transfer: his grief pours into her — centuries of it. His memories flood her: hunger, shame, loneliness, every forgotten face, every meal that cost him a piece of himself. She finally understands him completely. → *Intimate → overwhelmed.*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** Outside, the temple army presses; Sable and the Remnant hold the gate with Maren's borrowed grief-light. The rite must finish before dawn. → *Overwhelmed → desperate.*
- **Hook (E):** His memories flood her: hunger, shame, loneliness.

**Ch 34 — The Rite Complete (R5b)** *(Day 27→28 · 2,400 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+E):** The transfer ends. His curse breaks: the Devourer's form sloughs away — the vast shadow, the ash, the hunger — and a man remains: mortal, gaunt, real, breathing. → *Overwhelmed → awed.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** The cost is spoken plainly: she will never be numb again. She will feel everything — the world's grief, joy, pain, love — always, with no off-switch. She can still be touched, still be loved; she can never stop feeling. → *Awed → broken-open.*
- **Scene 3 (A+E):** He takes her hand — and it doesn't hurt him anymore. *"I can feel your hand,"* he says, wondering. She can still feel his hand in hers. She always will. → *Broken-open → tender.*
- **Hook (E):** She can still feel his hand in hers. She always will.

**Ch 35 ◆ Respite — The Rebranding** *(Day 28–29 · 2,400 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** The lull. The temple army halts — the "Devourer" is gone; the miracle is real. The dead are buried; Sable formally rebels before the soldiers, tearing her sisterhood oath. The Remnant dares to hope. → *Tender → bonded.*
- **Scene 2 (A+W):** The hope dies fast: the Matriarch rebrands Elowen as the *new* monster — "the bearer who stole the Devourer's power" — and orders the purge to continue. The army reforms. → *Bonded → sickened.*
- **Scene 3 (A+E):** Sable stands with Elowen, publicly, blade out: the alliance holds. But the purge-fire is being aimed at Elowen — at all the world's grief, hers now. → *Sickened → grim.*
- **Hook (C):** The purge-fire is aimed at her — at all the world's grief.

**Ch 36 — The Stand: The Lie Shatters** *(Day 29 · 2,500 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** The Matriarch weaponizes the purge-fire to burn away Elowen's burden — which means burning away the world's grief, i.e., everyone's emotional memory. The fire rises. Maren — mortal now, killable — steps in front of Elowen. She refuses to let him die for her. → *Grim → desperate.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** She steps past him, into the fire's path, and speaks the full true vow aloud, to everyone — temple soldiers, Remnant, the whole field: *"Let one bear all sorrow so the many may live. And the one who bears shall not bear alone."* The lie shatters. People turn. → *Desperate → luminous.*
- **Scene 3 (A+W):** The Matriarch's last gambit fails; her own fire turns on her, and she falls consumed, cursing: *"You'll become the next me."* The temple army breaks. → *Luminous → emptied.*
- **Hook (C):** The Matriarch falls into her own fire: "You'll become the next me."

**Ch 37 — The Sharing** *(Day 29 · 2,400 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+E):** After-fire. Elowen's answer to the Matriarch's curse: she *shares* the grief out — piece by piece, person by person. Every person takes a small weight, so the fire has no single target, and no one drowns. → *Emptied → filled.*
- **Scene 2 (A+W):** The mechanics of sharing: she teaches them to *listen* — the Remnant, the defected soldiers, the villagers. Grief moves from one to many; the world begins to carry itself again. → *Filled → awed.*
- **Scene 3 (A+E):** She is exhausted, raw, still feeling everything — but not alone. The weight is no longer hers alone. → *Awed → held.*
- **Hook (S):** The world begins to carry itself — will the sharing hold?

**Ch 38 — The First Kiss (Climax Internal / R6)** *(Day 29, night · 2,500 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+E):** After the battle. The Heart, quiet, ash settling. Maren approaches her — mortal, warm, breakable. He believed undoing the curse meant his death; he came to say goodbye. → *Held → aching.*
- **Scene 2 (E):** He offers her his mortality — *"I choose to feel. Because feeling has you in it."* She accepts — not as a Saint, as herself. → *Aching → tender.*
- **Scene 3 (E):** The first kiss — his goodbye-gift, meant as an ending; she uses it to refuse his ending, pouring the choice into it. After the kiss she looks at her hands: she still feels everything — his mouth, the world's grief — and she is not numb, and she is not dead. She is free. → *Tender → free.*
- **Hook (E):** After the kiss: she still feels everything. She is not numb. She is free.

**Ch 39 — Ordinary Life** *(Days 40–60 · 2,300 w)*
- **Scene 1 (A+W):** The temple falls as an institution. The Remnant founds a new community — not a temple, but a *shared* body: people take turns *listening* to grief, bearing it together. Sable leads; Niamh arrives with the last records, and Elowen forgives her, and is forgiven. → *Free → warm.*
- **Scene 2 (A+E):** Elowen and Maren learn ordinary life: he tastes sweetness for the first time (honey, rain, bread), laughs, lives without hunger. She watches him learn joy. → *Warm → light.*
- **Scene 3 (E):** Night. He asks if she regrets it — the transfer, the never-numb, the life she lost. *"No. I'm just still learning not to call it a curse."* → *Light → whole.*
- **Hook (E):** "Do you regret it?" — "No. I'm learning not to call it a curse."

**Ch 40 — The Garden of the Shared (Final Image)** *(~2 months later · 2,300 w)*
- **Scene 1 (W+E):** Mirror of Ch 1: Elowen in a garden at dawn — but this garden belongs to the new community; children run; grief is listened to, not sealed. She hears the world's grief — and she is no longer afraid of it. → *Whole → serene.*
- **Scene 2 (E):** Maren sits beside her — mortal, aging, feeling. A child falls and cries. Elowen does not take the grief away; she kneels and cries with the child. The child stops. Maren smiles. → *Serene → full.*
- **Scene 3 (E):** The last image: together, they bear — never alone. The Matriarch's curse is answered: she is not the next Matriarch, because she is never alone. HEA. → *Full → whole.*
- **Hook (E, thematic bookend):** Together, they bear — never alone.
